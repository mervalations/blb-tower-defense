You're a gambler in the casino. After every hand of poker, looters come to steal your winnings. Your cards become towers. Defend your stash. The biggest pile of gold after level 30 wins.

How to Play

  1. Deal. Each hand, click 5 spots on the felt to deal 5 cards. Every card is a tower. Put them where they cover your carpet (your lane has your colour).
  2. Swap (once). Select up to 4 cards you don't want and press Swap to replace them.
  3. Combine. Select cards that make a poker hand and press Combine. Your whole hand turns into one much stronger tower, built where the first card you selected stood.
  4. Defend. Looters run your maze toward your vault. Kills pay gold. Leaks cost gold. Hit 0 gold and you're out.
  5. Win the hand. The best poker hand each round wins bonus gold.
  6. Tip the dealer (10 gold each, before your first card) for a chance at a stacked deck. More tips, better odds.
  7. Fold to skip a round: no cards, but no looters either.
